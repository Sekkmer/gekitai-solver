How to play

Place a piece on an empty square. Adjacent pieces are pushed one space outward in all eight directions if unblocked. Pieces pushed off the board return to their owner. Win with three adjacent pieces in a straight line, with no gaps (including diagonals), or all eight pieces on the board. Both players winning at once is a draw.

The verified strategy covers every legal reply from the empty board. The 25-placement bound counts both players’ moves. Your game runs entirely in this browser.
