How to play

Place a piece on an empty square. Adjacent pieces are pushed one space outward in all eight directions if unblocked. Pieces pushed off the board return to their owner. Win with three in a row or all eight pieces on the board. Both players winning at once is a draw.

A verified strategy covers every legal reply from its starting position. The bound counts both players’ placements; it does not claim the shortest possible win. One shared game per server.
